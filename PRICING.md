## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

A: Yes, you can upgrade at any time. Downgrades are available at contract renewal.

### Q: What happens if I exceed my stream limit?

A: Growth Tier customers will be notified when approaching their limit. Temporary overages are allowed but sustained overages require an upgrade.

### Q: Do you offer educational discounts?

A: Yes, educational institutions receive 20% off all plans. Contact us for verification.

### Q: Can I self-host with the Growth Tier?

A: Self-hosting is available only with the Enterprise Tier due to the complexity of deployment and support requirements.

### Q: What compliance certifications do you support?

A: Enterprise Tier includes documentation and support for HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, FedRAMP, and PCI DSS compliance.

### Q: How long is the contract term?

A: All plans are annual. Multi-year contracts are available with discounts for Enterprise Tier.
